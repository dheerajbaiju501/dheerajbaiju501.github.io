Dheeraj Baiju

Previously, I was a researcher at IISc Bangalore with Prof. Anirban Chakraborty, where I worked on developing multimodal frameworks for holistic evaluation of text-to-image generative models.

I've also collaborated with Prof. Narendra Ahuja, where I worked on leveraging semantic attribute-level reasoning from multimodal models to improve fine-grained visual retrieval. Previously, I worked with Dr. Prashant Kumar on point cloud completion, exploring how topological priors can improve reconstruction from incomplete real-world 3D scans.

I completed my B.E. in Electrical and Electronics Engineering at BITS Pilani, where I worked with Prof. Tejasvi Alladi.

Beyond research. I play football and squash, enjoy long-distance running, and recently returned to the piano after a long break.
