Dheeraj Baiju

Previously, I was a researcher at IISc Bangalore, working with Prof. Anirban Chakraborty on developing a multimodal framework for evaluating text-to-image generative models. I also collaborated with Prof. Narendra Ahuja on improving fine-grained visual retrieval using semantic reasoning from multimodal models. I did my undergrad at BITS Pilani where I majored in Electrical and Electronics Engineering and did research on topology-aware 3D point cloud completion.

Beyond research. I play football and squash, enjoy long-distance running, and recently returned to the piano after a long break.
